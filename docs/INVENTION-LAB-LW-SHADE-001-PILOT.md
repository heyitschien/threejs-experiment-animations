## Proposed v1 fixture
All lengths in mm, right-handed CAD X=slat span, Y=chord/depth, Z=vertical; +X pivot axes. Ground pivots in the linkage side plane at (Y,Z)=(0,0),(0,80); two 300×60×4 slat envelopes centered on their shaft axes. Two rigid equal crank arms, 20 mm center-to-center, and one 80 mm center-to-center coupler, forming a parallelogram with the fixed ground. One control angle theta=0..60° (physical CAD rotation -theta about +X to match existing window convention); nominal 25°, named 0°,25°,60°. At theta, crank ends in side-plane coordinates C=(20 cos theta,-20 sin theta), D=(20 cos theta,80-20 sin theta). D-C=(0,80); arms remain parallel and coupler vertical. Test theta 0..60 inclusive at 5° spacing (13 poses); 90° is collinear and explicitly outside the permitted range. Both slats must be driven by the same one-degree-of-freedom mechanism, not arbitrary independent animations. No motor or electronics.

Initial joint concept: 3.0 mm pin shaft, 3.4 mm bore (0.20 mm radial clearance), arm/coupler plate thickness 3 mm, 0.50 mm minimum noncontact axial gap between moving plates where appropriate. Hardware candidate: plain M3 washer, nominal ID 3.2, OD 7, thickness 0.5 mm, verify actual catalog dimensions and model; size is a proposed match, not a supplier specification. Model side supports, pin/shaft, crank-to-slat attachment, coupler, retained pin/washer stack with named parts and explicit signed poses. Locate linkage outboard of the slat ends, axially stagger overlapping links; freeze exact support offsets, axial stack, hole centers and intended contact exceptions before generating geometry. Use simplest valid pin/washer geometry if catalog unavailable. Design dimensions above are conceptual digital inputs, not manufacture tolerances. Do not claim retention, structural or environmental performance. Preserve fixed part and distinct moving parts.
