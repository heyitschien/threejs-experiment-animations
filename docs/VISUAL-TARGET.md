## Motion and framing

Use slow body drift, phase-offset traveling waves and delayed tip follow-through. Check the swept profile in a 15-second sample for root sliding, abrupt roll, popping, interpenetration and synchronized motion. Inspect side and rear angles for hidden assembly issues. Preserve pause/resume, camera reset and reduced-motion behavior.

Frame the entire silhouette at 390 × 844 without control overlap and maintain a clean text zone at 1440 × 900. Use a restrained low three-quarter angle for the hero and a neutral front view to count arms. Keep the reactor and optic distinct in both. Limit visual noise behind the outline. Use original procedural geometry throughout the baseline pipeline.

## Independent review rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 3. A score of 0 means broken or absent, 1 means substantial defects, 2 means clearly readable with specific remaining flaws, and 3 means the cue holds across required shots.

- **Silhouette:** eight attached arms, controlled spacing, distinct foreground and rear curves, clean taper.
- **Mantle:** integrated neck and shell, legible optic and reactor, believable seams.
- **Materials and lighting:** silver chrome bands with dark intervals, controlled glow, readable violet and amber accents, depth cues.
- **Motion:** stable roots, continuous bending and distinct phases.
- **Composition:** whole subject and controls remain readable on both sizes.

A phase candidate needs at least 2 on its targeted dimension, improvement over its own baseline, no drop in other scored dimensions, and a pass on integrity, interaction and performance gates. Record visible evidence for each score and name which cues remain unverified.
